Abaqus Training for Engineering Students

Stuck on a mesh, unsure which boundary conditions to apply, or trying to work out why your job aborted? Desyngo helps engineering students build and troubleshoot Abaqus models and explain what their simulation results mean.

Ask about Abaqus training

Build your first model or work through a problem

The training is intended for engineering students learning FEA, including BTech and MTech students working on coursework or projects. If you already have a model, tell us the step where you got stuck so we can discuss the right starting point.

  • Set up a model. Define materials, loads and boundary conditions for a simple structural problem.
  • Build and check a mesh. Choose an element type and mesh size, troubleshoot meshing errors, and compare results after refining the mesh.
  • Run and troubleshoot a job. Read job messages and check the setup for likely causes of errors.
  • Interpret results. Read stress and displacement plots and explain what the model assumptions mean for your conclusions.

Start with our free introduction to Abaqus and FEA, which shows a bracket at the geometry, mesh and results stages.
