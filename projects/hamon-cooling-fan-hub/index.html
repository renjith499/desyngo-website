Fan Hub Design for Hamon Cooling

Desyngo developed a fan hub design for Hamon Cooling. The project focused on vibration reduction, efficiency and mechanical robustness.

The design challenge

A fan hub connects the blades to the drive shaft and transfers loads through the rotating assembly. The design needs to support those loads while limiting vibration during operation. Changes to geometry and mass distribution can affect both stiffness and vibration response.

What to consider on a similar project

Start with the operating speed range, blade and hub geometry, connection details and the conditions under which vibration occurs. These inputs help define the load cases and determine whether stress, deformation or vibration response needs closer evaluation.
