Structural Analysis and FEA Services

Desyngo CAE Solutions LLP uses finite element analysis (FEA) to evaluate how industrial, marine and aerospace components respond to their operating loads. We help engineering teams understand where a design may need reinforcement and compare options before fabrication.

Request an FEA quote

What we can help you evaluate

  • Industrial equipment. Assess the stiffness and load paths of components, frames and supports under operating loads.
  • Marine structures. Evaluate how equipment housings and assemblies respond to pressure and support loads.
  • Aerospace components. Compare design options when weight, stiffness and strength all matter.

See how these design questions apply to rotating equipment in our Hamon Cooling fan hub project.

What to send for a quote

Start with a short description of the design decision you need to make. If available, include drawings or CAD, material information, expected loads, mounting details and any acceptance criteria. Let us know the project timeline and which inputs still need to be confirmed.

What an analysis can deliver

We agree on the analysis scope with you before work begins. Depending on the project, the report can document the model assumptions and load cases, present stress and displacement results, and explain how they affect the design decision. When comparing options, we can summarize the trade-offs and flag uncertainties that need further testing or input.
